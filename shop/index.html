/The typeface

Every pixel is a circle. A monospace display typeface on a 5×7 grid, where the weight axis grows the dots instead of thickening the strokes. Separated points at Thin, overlapping letterforms at Black.

Fully monospace, so every glyph shares a width and text locks to a grid. Made for code-style layouts, tickers, posters and motion.
