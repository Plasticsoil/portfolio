    moji

    A tiny daily word game — one cryptic clue a day, told mostly in emoji.

    How it works

    Each clue hides two things at once: a plain definition, and a bit of wordplay built from the emoji — sounds, anagrams, words tucked inside other words. Both point to the same answer. Type it on the keyboard, beat the clock, and publish your time to the day's leaderboard.

    One a day

    There's a single puzzle each day, the same for everyone. Once you've solved it, that's it until tomorrow — come back for a fresh clue.

    The clues

    They're cryptic, so the emoji are rarely literal. 🧈 + 🪰 isn't an insect — it's butter + fly → BUTTERFLY. Read them out loud and play with the sounds.

    The small print

    moji is just a personal experiment — me vibecoding a little game for the fun of it. No warranties, no prizes, no stakes, and nothing here is guaranteed to work or to stick around. Play purely for enjoyment: it's offered exactly as-is, and it's not something anyone could be held liable for. 🙂
