    moji

    A daily word puzzle with a twist. One clue a day, and every clue is a little riddle that hides its answer twice over.

    How it works

    Each clue looks like an ordinary sentence, but it secretly does two jobs at once. One part is a plain definition of the answer. The other part is wordplay: a sneaky recipe that spells the same answer out a different way. Crack either side and you're in.

    Seeing through it

    The trap is that the definition can sit at the start or the end, with nothing to show where it stops and the wordplay begins. Little signal words give the game away. "Mixed" or "broken" usually means the letters are scrambled. "In" or "held by" means one word is tucked inside another. "We hear" means it sounds like something else.

    One a day

    There is a single puzzle each day, the same for everyone. Solve it, drop your name on the board, then come back tomorrow for a fresh one.

    The emoji

    Sometimes an emoji slips into a clue. It might just be a picture of a word, or it might be pulling its weight, standing in for a letter or a sound. Read it out loud and see what it turns into.

    The small print

    moji is a personal experiment, just me building a little game for the fun of it. No promises, no prizes, nothing to win or lose. It is offered exactly as it is, purely to enjoy, and it is not something anyone could be held to account for. Have fun.
