    moji

    A daily word puzzle with a twist. Three clues a day, each one a little riddle that hides its answer twice over.

    How it works

    Each clue looks like an ordinary sentence, but it secretly does two jobs at once. One part is a plain definition of the answer. The other part is wordplay: a sneaky recipe that spells the same answer out a different way. Crack either side and you're in.

    Seeing through it

    The trap is that the definition can sit at the start or the end, with nothing to show where it stops and the wordplay begins. Little signal words give the game away. "Mixed" or "broken" usually means the letters are scrambled. "In" or "held by" means one word is tucked inside another. "We hear" means it sounds like something else.

    Three a day

    Each day is a little ladder of three clues that climb from gentle to fiendish. The first is a warm up, where two emoji fuse into one word. The second is a short three letter teaser. The third is a proper cryptic knot. The clock runs across all three, the set is the same for everyone, and the day is yours once you reach the top. Drop your name on the board, then come back tomorrow for a fresh climb.

    The tag

    A small coloured label in the corner names the trick at play: an anagram, a hidden word, a homophone, a container, and so on. It is a hint, not a spoiler. Each kind has its own colour, so you start to recognise them by sight.

    The emoji

    Every clue carries at least one emoji. Sometimes it is just a picture of a word, and sometimes it is pulling its weight, standing in for a letter or a sound. Read it out loud and see what it turns into.

    The small print

    moji is a personal experiment, just me building a little game for the fun of it. No promises, no prizes, nothing to win or lose. It is offered exactly as it is, purely to enjoy, and it is not something anyone could be held to account for. Have fun.
